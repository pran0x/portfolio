Who I Am

My work centers on web application security and penetration testing — rooted in deep enumeration, trust boundary analysis, and adversary simulation. I don't stop at finding a vulnerability. I follow the chain until the full impact is clear.

From outside the security scope — I am Pritom Pranto, aka pran0x, a 22-year-old security enthusiast from Bangladesh.

I started in security through CTFs and bug bounties, but quickly realized my passion was in the research itself — understanding how things break, not just that they do. This led me to focus on penetration testing and vulnerability research as a career.
